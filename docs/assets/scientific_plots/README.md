# UrjaKavach Scientific Visualizations & Research Figures
## Grade J304 Stainless Slab — Pyrometallurgical & Emission Distributions

This directory indexes the peer-reviewed grade metallurgical figures generated for Round 2 presentation and research publication:

### 1. Cost-Carbon Pareto Optimization Curves
- `scientific_j304_pareto_with_legend.png`: 50-point continuous HiGHS Simplex Pareto frontier trading off operating charge cost (\$USD/t finished slab) against Total GHG Intensity ($\text{tCO}_2/\text{t}$). Highlights the UrjaKavach sweet spot ($0.820\text{ tCO}_2/\text{t}$) versus the historical baseline ($2.285\text{ tCO}_2/\text{t}$).
- `scientific_j304_pareto_no_legend.png`: Clean presentation variant without legend overlay.
- `r2_j304_pareto_scatter.png`: High-density scatter plot with secondary axis tracking recycled scrap charge fraction ($w_{\text{scrap}}$).

### 2. Scrap Charge Sensitivity Boxplots
- `scientific_j304_boxplot_with_legend.png`: Multi-grade variance distribution across 1,000 Monte Carlo heats under varying raw material tramp impurity drift.
- `scientific_j304_boxplot_no_legend.png`: Unannotated boxplot variant for executive slides.

### 3. Slag Basicity vs. Chromium Recovery Heatmaps
- `scientific_j304_heatmap_with_legend.png`: 2D thermodynamic matrix showing quaternary basicity ($B_2 = 1.5\text{--}1.8$) versus bath tapping temperature, highlighting the $>92\%$ Cr reduction recovery zone.
- `scientific_j304_heatmap_no_legend.png`: Minimalist heatmap variant.
